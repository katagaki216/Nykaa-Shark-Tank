01 • ACCOUNTING REALITY

Brand Accounting: The Accounting Reality

Ind AS 38 specifically states that internally generated brands are not recognised as intangible assets because their cost cannot be distinguished from the cost of developing the business as a whole.

◈

INTANGIBLE ASSET

A brand has no physical form and may generate future economic benefits.

▣

ACCOUNTING RULE

Ind AS 38 paragraph 63: internally generated brands, mastheads, publishing titles and customer lists are not recognised as intangible assets.

02 • VALUATION METHOD

How Do We Value the Brand?

01

Cost Approach

Considers the cost to recreate or replace a similar brand.

02

Market Approach

Uses comparable brand transactions where reliable evidence exists.

03 • Selected

Royalty Relief

Values the brand by estimating the present value of royalties avoided through ownership.

Revenue→Royalty Rate→Royalty Saving→After-tax Saving→PV + Terminal Value→Brand Value
Brand Value = PV of forecast after-tax royalty savings + PV of terminal value
Terminal Value = Year 5 after-tax royalty saving × (1 + terminal growth) ÷ (discount rate − terminal growth)
